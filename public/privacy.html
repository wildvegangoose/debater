Privacy Policy

Steelman (io.dialectica.debater) · Effective November 26, 2026

Steelman is an argument-mapping and debate sparring application designed with local-first privacy. No account or sign-in is required to use the app.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Debate history & Performance Logs: Your sparring transcripts, scores, deep critiques, custom sandbox nodes, and settings are stored locally in your device’s browser/app storage (localStorage) and are never uploaded to Steelman servers.
  • API keys: If you choose to connect your own Google Gemini API key or local OpenAI-compatible server, those credentials remain solely on your device and are sent only to the AI endpoint you configure.
  • On-Device AI (WebLLM): When enabled, open-weight model files are cached locally on your device so inference runs privately without sending prompts to external servers.

2. Product Analytics & Feedback

Steelman uses PostHog to collect anonymized product usage metrics (such as which screens, topics, and features are used, round completion counts, and error types) so we can improve the app.

  • We do not send your private debate arguments, transcripts, or API keys to PostHog. Text inputs and user debate bubbles are masked in session replays.
  • If you voluntarily submit a note through the in-app Feedback modal, the sentiment, category, message text, and active screen/topic are sent to PostHog so we can review and act on your suggestion.

3. Device Permissions (Android)

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Optional, used only when you actively dictate arguments. Audio is processed by your device’s speech recognition service and is not stored by Steelman.
  • Internet: Used to load app updates, optional cloud AI responses (if you configure Gemini or a LAN server), and anonymized analytics.

4. Data Deletion

Because Steelman does not maintain user accounts or cloud databases for your debates, you can delete all local data at any time by clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings (or browser site data), or by deleting individual rounds in the Performance Log.

5. Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be opened at github.com/wildvegangoose/debater/issues.
